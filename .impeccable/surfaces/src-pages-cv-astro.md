# Printed CV (src/pages/cv.astro)

## Scope and mode

The CV: one long page at `/cv`, built to print to A4 and to be read on screen by anyone who opens it. Visitor mode: **Persuade**. The website at `/` links here for "CV"; this page's CV button saves it as a PDF until the owner adds a PDF file.

## Audience, job, action

- Recruiters and internship coordinators, who receive it attached to an application or print it.
- Hiring engineers, who check the measured characteristics against the public repositories.
- Primary actions: email, and saving or downloading the CV.

## Proof and content

- **Typical application (Section 6): Specify**, the owner's choice: an AI email and recruitment assistant, told as one deep case study counted at commit `e537860`.
- RWBY Afterlight and Study Hub appear as compact entries under Other Applications.
- Skills are pins that link to the exact source files across the three public repositories.
- Identity, availability and certificate values that are still unknown stay placeholders, flagged with the datasheet's TBD vocabulary.

## Constraints

- No invented employers, metrics, testimonials or certificates.
- Light paper only, and it prints cleanly to A4.
- WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard-complete, reduced motion respected.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The owner, specified like an electronic part. Features, pin functions, recommended operating conditions, measured characteristics and a typical application, every line checkable. It refuses the category default: a dark hero with a gradient, percentage skill bars and a grid of same-size project cards.

OWN-WORLD:
- Ground and ink: datasheet paper `#fbfbfa` and black ink `#151515`.
- Accent: one manufacturer red `#c8102e`, for actions, numbers, status flags and pin highlights.
- Neutrals: instrument grey `#8a8f94` for rules, plus tinted table heads.
- Type: Archivo on its width axis, with Fragment Mono for codes and values.
- Components: numbered sections, ruled tables with footnotes, numbered figures, running heads and page breaks, a 16-pin DIP pinout, a functional block diagram and an ordering-information table.

STORY: In ten seconds the reader knows who this is, what they are studying, when they are free, and that they ship real full-stack and AI software. In five minutes an engineer has opened a pin's source file and read how Specify classifies email without ever sending any. Then they email or keep the PDF.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- The product-folder bar: monogram, name, section links, CV and Email.
- The running head, then the name in display-size expanded Archivo beside the part box with `STATUS: AVAILABLE`.
- Then Features, Applications and Description in three columns, with Figure 1 (Specify drawn as U1) starting at the fold.

FORM: Component datasheet, position 1 on the original ordered list, chosen by the user as IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the assigned departures board. Seed key 28002b5d. It was kept as the printed-CV format when the website moved to the Poster Session.
- Signature interaction: the pinout. Hovering or focusing a pin draws a red trace and lights its Pin Functions row.
- Motion grammar: the pins seat once, in sequence, when the pinout first enters view.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
